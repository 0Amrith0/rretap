## Overview

Amazon DSP is an omnichannel demand-side platform that lets advertisers programmatically buy audio, display, online video, Streaming TV, and physical store advertising, using Amazon's first-party data and AI-powered automation to target audiences even without third-party cookies. It's available to brand advertisers, agencies, and tool providers, whether or not they sell on Amazon, via self-service or a managed-service option (typically requiring a $50,000 USD minimum spend), and offers audience targeting across Amazon, advertiser, and third-party data sources. Amazon has also introduced a centralized hub unifying access to all Amazon Ads products, bringing sponsored ads and Amazon DSP workflows into a single workspace, and DSP includes integrated media planning tools, multiple programmatic buying options, and standard plus exclusive Amazon creative formats. DSP campaigns are measured through the same reporting tools covered in [Reporting Optimization](Reporting-Optimization.md), follow [budget and placement](Budget-Placement.md) mechanics similar to Sponsored ad types, and sit alongside [Sponsored Display](Sponsored-Display.md) within Amazon's unified display ads offering.

- Amazon DSP ads can appear on first-party Amazon-owned properties such as Prime Video, Twitch, live sports (e.g. Thursday Night Football), Amazon.com, Amazon Fresh kiosks, Fire TV, Kindle, and Alexa, as well as on third-party sites and apps via Amazon Publisher Direct and third-party exchanges, though availability may vary by region. — [source](https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp), confidence: High, last-checked: 2026-09-07
- Amazon DSP is available to brand advertisers, ad agencies, and tool providers, and can be used by both advertisers who sell products on Amazon and those who do not. — [source](https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp), confidence: High, last-checked: 2026-09-07
- A demand-side platform (DSP) is a tool that automates and centralizes media buying from multiple sources to streamline advertising for greater efficiency and reach. — [source](https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp), confidence: High, last-checked: 2026-09-07
- Amazon DSP offers two ways to get started: self-service, where customers fully control their own campaigns, and managed-service, which is designed for advertisers wanting consultative service or with limited programmatic experience and typically requires a minimum spend of $50,000 USD (minimum may vary by country). — [source](https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp), confidence: High, last-checked: 2026-09-07
- Amazon DSP offers audience targeting in three main categories: Amazon Audiences (based on first-party buying, browsing, and streaming signals), advertiser audiences (driven by custom inputs like product relevance and remarketing), and third-party audiences (sourced from broader internet activities such as car purchases or cable subscriptions). — [source](https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp), confidence: High, last-checked: 2026-09-07
- Amazon DSP provides an integrated media planning suite with tools like a cross-channel planner and channel planner that help allocate media across Amazon DSP supply and simulate reach and budget in real time based on live inventory across linear TV, streaming TV, and audio. — [source](https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp), confidence: High, last-checked: 2026-09-07
- Amazon DSP offers programmatic media buying options including real-time bidding (RTB), private marketplaces (PMP), private auction, and programmatic guaranteed buying, along with various deals-based options, and an inventory hub that centralizes deals across Amazon properties and third-party inventory. — [source](https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp), confidence: High, last-checked: 2026-09-07
- Amazon DSP supports standard IAB creative sizes, custom-designed ads, and exclusive Amazon creative options such as e-commerce creative templates that can use an advertiser-provided image or a product image from the product detail page. — [source](https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-dsp), confidence: High, last-checked: 2026-09-07
