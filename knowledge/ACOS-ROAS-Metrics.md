## Overview

ACoS (Advertising Cost of Sales) measures how many dollars of revenue resulted from each dollar spent on an ad campaign, calculated by dividing total ad spend by total attributed ad sales and expressing the result as a percentage. Break-even ACoS — profit divided by revenue (sales price minus total product cost and Amazon's fees) — represents the maximum percentage of the sales price that can be spent on PPC before the seller starts losing money. Beyond ACoS/ROAS, Amazon also tracks new-to-brand metrics — first-time order counts and sales from customers new to a brand within a 12-month look-back window — and recommends using them alongside ACoS or ROAS, since brand-growth campaigns (such as those often run via Sponsored Brands) that drive first-time purchases can cost more than repeat-purchase campaigns, a nuance ACoS or ROAS alone would not capture.
